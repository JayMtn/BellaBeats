Conclusions

  • Although HeartRate and Sleep had the most entries; the Steps, calories and activity sections had a higher amount of participants indicating a stronger usage in these categories.
  • Light Activity produced had the highest mean compared to FairlyActive and VeryActive. This could be because of their individual profession where they spend most of their time.
  • Data also shows a high amount of SedentaryMinutes with avg. 995 which means most participants were active only 31% of their day.
  • 24hrs a day x 60minutes in an hour = 1440 minutes. 995min/1440Min = .69. 1-.69=.31
  • utilize the sedetary minutes block to release “targeted campaings/promotions” while you have the users attention.
  • Bellabeat has incurred a fast growth rate and has appealed to several women across the globe.

The following are insights on what we would recommend moving forward into next year, promoting Growth and Challenges for current customers.

  • Bella users are more active on Saturday and Wednesday with Saturday showing the highest amount of activity. We also see most participants were involved in LightActivity, this could be due to time constraints or personal schedules. Would be beneficial to raise awareness on app of lightactvity related competitions during the week and have a heavier activity challenge on Saturday being the busiest day.
  • Each graph on calories and activity shows us the more active users are, the more calories they burn. This has been known to support good health.
  • Launching a campaign for users to join together to win “belladollars”, prizes or even trips; to help raise awareness and increase enrolled users under the bellabeat platform.
  • Earlier we found that, on average, users are only active for under 35% of their time in a 24hour period. Note we would expect this to increase a little as 8hrs (30%) of their time is reserved for sleeping- therefore there is room for increased activity for all bella users.
  • There is several opportunities for both bella and its users to improve as the market and usage is strong. The fit bit is popular and users are wearing them even if they are not active, maybe offer links/appointment calls with life coaches, workout plans, health/eating plans and competitions to help raise awareness and increase activity which in turn produces healthier clients.
  • Start thinking of quarterly competitions and maybe have an annual retreat for your top performers per region as a reward for their usage which can be tracked by a point based system.
  • Bella has a strong influence amongst women and opportunity to grow even more within the active health industry.
